In collaboration with Victoria Queer Boxing, we'll be hosting our Beginner's Night on November 22nd!


Starting at 4:00, we will have a number of speakers, some live sparring, and a chance for anyone in attendance (but especially beginners) to try out kickboxing with one of our many coaches!


No skill level is required! If you are interested in what we do, this is a great chance to get a look at what it's like!
