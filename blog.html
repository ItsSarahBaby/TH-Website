Nov 19, 2026

That rally we've been organizing is tomorrow.

Like whatttt are we even doing here!!! The Conservative supermajority was sworn in TODAY. They are poised to begin their attacks on SOGI TOMORROW. I seriously thought we had a little more time than this.


I feel so unprepared as an organized movement. So much frustration has been repeatedly channeled into liberal ass institutional grinding that has yet to yield any results besides Eby's feckless NDP and their generationally pathetic run this past election cycle (Grace Lore you're one of the good ones). And now, we find ourselves here. The real attacks are beginning, and it feels like there is a culture of complacency leftover from how many decades without a Conservative majority government?


And that's the kicker! Conservatives can do a hell of a lot of damage, but until it gets real bad, they target it in ways where the average person isn't going to feel a thing. We're likely going to face a situation where the everyday people in this province "know it's bad", but don't do anything to change it - out of apathy, avoidance, or maybe they just enjoy the scapegoating.


But maybe a common enemy will be good for the activist scene in this city. My hope is that it clears our eyes. The people I've been lucky enough to work with the past minute give me hope that we are starting to move away from those hyperindividualist self-flagellating COINTELPRO neuroses that characterize so much of activist spaces. Certainly, I feel more like there are concrete steps being taken. I guess it's really just about putting on a successful rally tomorrow.


wml. I'm so nervous!


October 26, 2026

I'm just building the website today. I'm having a lot of fun with it! It's been a long time since I've worked with HTML, but I'm really getting that Web 1.0 look I was hoping for.


Plenty of things are in the works right now. November's looking like a busy month! Hopefully I will have more updates on the way soon. This website was made with the express purpose of helping organize these upcoming events, so we'll see if it dies after that work has been finished. Hard to say.


Anyway, it's nice to have a spot on the internet that feels like it's actually mine again. I'll likely end up using this blog page for more informal updates and personal news, so if you're interested in my goings-on, I guess this will be the place. I find it very easy to write for this format, so we'll see if I get in the habit of updating it.
